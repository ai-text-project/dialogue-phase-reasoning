## Observed Emergence

Community Mapping proceeded through several stages:

Community Taxonomy

↓

Alignment Criteria

↓

Community Inventory

↓

Community Structure Mapping

↓

Asset-to-Community Mapping

During this process, a structural shift was observed.

Initial framing:

↓

Reasoning Community

Later interpretation:

↓

Human-AI Collaboration

↓

Knowledge Organization

↓

Research Infrastructure

↓

Research Program Evolution

The mapping exercise increasingly suggested that the research program could not be understood solely as a reasoning-oriented project.

Instead, a substantial portion of the existing assets appeared to relate to:

* research formation
* knowledge organization
* research-space construction
* research program evolution
* meta-level observation

The exploration of external communities unexpectedly produced a reinterpretation of the internal research structure.

The output did not primarily consist of candidate communities.

The investigation of external structures functioned as a mirror through which existing assets became reorganized.

---

## Self-Observation Commentary

The most notable feature was not the discovery of new information.

Rather, previously existing assets were repositioned within a newly constructed structural frame.

The observed phenomenon appears closer to:

* structural reconfiguration
* reframing
* topological reinterpretation

than to direct theory generation.

At present, the observation is insufficient to classify as a clear intelligence-emergence-like phenomenon.

However, it appears to be a strong candidate for:

Research Program Reinterpretation through Community Mapping.

Further validation after a crystallization interval is recommended.
