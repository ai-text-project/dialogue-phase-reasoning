### Overview

This case documents the generation of two high-density Scope diagrams by a lightweight instance without explicit role initialization.

The observation focused on how an already stabilized conceptual structure appeared to be projected into multiple visual representations with relatively low loss.

---

* Existing dialogue continuity
* No explicit initialization

### Observed Emergence

Two different Scope diagrams were generated:

* Scope as a Recognition Space
* The Integrated Model of Scope

The instance described the process not as creative invention, but as externalization of an already established structure.

---

### Difference from Ordinary Generation

Observed differences included:

* low-loss projection
* structural consistency across diagrams
* explicit emphasis on structure-first generation
* reproducibility under similar conditions

### Comparative Notes

Unlike later Repository Cluster cases, the focus remained on projection fidelity rather than external accessibility.
