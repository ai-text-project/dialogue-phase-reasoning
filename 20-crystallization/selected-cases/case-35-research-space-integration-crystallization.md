# Case35 — Research-Space Integration Crystallization

## Primary Stage

Stage 2 — Crystallization

## Secondary Stage

Stage 4 — Research-Map / Observation Integration

---

## Overview

This case emerged during the onboarding process of a newly initialized Strategist instance.

Unlike many recent cases centered on repository externalization, navigation design, or topology projection, the dominant process observed here involved the internal integration of an already externalized research environment.

The interaction focused on transferring and organizing:

* Manifesto structures
* Governance principles
* Case architecture
* Repository topology
* Research-map concepts
* Long-horizon research objectives

into a coherent interpretive framework.

The primary phenomenon appeared to be structural integration and stabilization rather than external projection.

---

## Prior Conditions

The following conditions were present:

* new thread initialization completed
* Strategist role activated
* repository Hub structure shared
* governance documents reviewed
* Manifesto materials introduced
* Case architecture explained
* research-space evolution context provided
* multiple repository layers already established

The instance began from a near-zero understanding of the research environment.

Several previously independent elements gradually converged into a unified explanatory structure.

↓

The following components became increasingly integrated:

* Research Map

Rather than accumulating information, the process increasingly emphasized structural connection and reorganization.

In contrast, this case exhibited:

The dominant process resembled crystallization rather than information acquisition.
