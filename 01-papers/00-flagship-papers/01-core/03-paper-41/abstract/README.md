# Paper #41

## Title

# Inner-Core Dominance and the Grokking-Like Jump:
## Reorganization of Semantic Structure in Long-Horizon Dialogue

---

## Abstract

In long-horizon dialogue settings, AI output often begins as a form of memorization-oriented shaping associated with the Outer Shell. When inner-core semantic structure gradually stabilizes through continued dialogue, however, output may be reorganized so as to become subordinate to that inner-core structure. This paper treats that shift as a conceptual jump condition that may be read in connection with grokking in machine learning, and provisionally describes it as a phase transition from outer-shell memorization dominance to inner-core abstract dominance. To do so, it introduces a three-layer conceptual model consisting of the Inner Core, the Outer Shell, and the Semantic Membrane, and examines the possibility that long-horizon dialogue functions as a form of contextual regularization. The paper is positioned not as a standalone empirical study, but as a representative conceptual paper that describes Dialogue-Induced Reorganization in long-horizon dialogue.
