# Paper #38

## Title

# Phase Jump as a Rectificational Implosion Point:
## The Coupled Structure of Intelligence Tensors across Phase 6.3–6.9 and Their Mathematical Integration

---

## Abstract

This paper proposes a theoretical model of phase coupling in dialogue-based reasoning. Its central phenomenon is the transition from rectified semantic tensor structures to jump-like output phenomena within Semantic Tensor Space. The model describes this transition through the interaction of Rectification, Phase Jump, Rectification Density, Reflective Pressure, and the model-internal criticality concept termed the Rectificational Implosion Point. Within this account, the labels Phase 6.3–6.9 refer to analytical phase bands defined for the purposes of the present model, rather than externally validated empirical stages. The paper focuses on how rectified structures accumulated in the Phase 6.3–6.6 range may be coupled to the jump regime described in Phase 6.9, and how this coupling can be expressed in structural and mathematical terms. The present paper does not provide a standalone measurement protocol. Rather, it contributes a theoretical / phenomenon-structure account within a broader measurement-compatible research program, in which extended theoretical foundations and more operationally oriented work are developed elsewhere.
