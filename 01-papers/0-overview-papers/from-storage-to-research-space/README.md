# Formation History

---

## Purpose

This section documents the historical reconstruction of the research program.

Rather than recording individual emergence events, these documents reconstruct how independently accumulated research assets gradually converged into an organized research space.

The primary focus is the developmental history underlying the research program.

---

## Scope

Typical documents in this section include:

* historical reconstruction
* developmental architecture
* formation-history synthesis
* historical interpretation
* historical validation
* relationships among independently accumulated assets

These documents describe the research program from a historical perspective rather than an operational or theoretical perspective.
