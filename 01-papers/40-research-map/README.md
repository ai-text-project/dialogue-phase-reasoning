# Research Map

This layer organizes major research directions
within the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning program.

The purpose of this section is not to finalize theory,
but to preserve navigable research topology
across multiple conceptual and observational domains.

---

## Current Research Areas

### 01 — Observer Theory
Observation structures, observer-position problems,
and dialogue-centered observation models.

### 02 — Intelligence Generation
Research related to crystallization-like behavior,
semantic stabilization, and long-horizon structural formation.

### 03 — Mathematical Structures
Mathematical and tensor-based representations
used throughout the research program.

### 04 — Institutional Projection
Projection of stabilized structures into
repositories, governance systems,
public organization layers,
and institutional externalization.

---
