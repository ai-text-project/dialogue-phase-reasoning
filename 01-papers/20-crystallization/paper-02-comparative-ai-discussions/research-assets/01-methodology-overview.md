# Methodology Overview

## Purpose

This document provides a concise overview of the comparative methodology proposed in this study.

Rather than describing the complete research procedure in manuscript form, this overview explains the core methodological architecture supporting the research.

---

# Methodological Objective

The methodology aims to transform publicly available AI research discussions into reusable comparative research assets through structural observation.

Instead of comparing transcripts directly, the proposed approach introduces **Transcript Observation** as the primary unit of comparative analysis.

---

# Core Workflow

The methodology consists of the following stages.

```text
Public AI Research Discussions
        │
        ▼
Comparative Observation
        │
        ▼
Transcript Observation
        │
        ▼
Comparative Transcript Observation Corpus
        │
        ▼
Comparative Framework
        │
        ▼
Comparative Interpretation
```

Each stage contributes to the gradual transformation of publicly available discussions into reusable comparative research resources.

---

# Principal Methodological Components

The proposed methodology is organized around three interconnected components.

## Comparative Observation

Public discussions are examined through structural observation rather than transcript comparison.

Observation records capture recurring structural characteristics while minimizing dependence on narrative detail.

---

## Comparative Transcript Observation Corpus

Individual Observation records are accumulated into a reusable Observation Corpus.

The Corpus serves as the primary research resource supporting comparative analysis across multiple independent discussions.

---

## Comparative Framework

Comparative analysis of the Observation Corpus produces a provisional Comparative Framework.

The Framework synthesizes recurring structural relationships identified across the accumulated observations.

---

# Methodological Characteristics

The methodology has several distinguishing characteristics.

- Observation is treated as the primary comparative unit.
- Public discussions are transformed into reusable research assets.
- The Observation Corpus supports cumulative comparative research.
- Comparative interpretation is grounded in systematically organized observations rather than isolated transcripts.
