# Observation Workflow

## Purpose

This document describes the operational workflow through which publicly available AI research discussions are transformed into reusable comparative research assets.

Rather than presenting methodological theory, this document focuses on the sequence of research activities performed throughout the observation process.

---

# Observation Workflow

The proposed workflow consists of six consecutive stages.

```text
Public AI Research Discussions
        │
        ▼
Comparative Observation
        │
        ▼
Transcript Observation
        │
        ▼
Comparative Transcript Observation Corpus
        │
        ▼
Comparative Framework
        │
        ▼
Comparative Interpretation
```

Each stage produces research assets that support the following stage.

---

# Stage 1 — Public AI Research Discussions

The workflow begins with publicly available AI research discussions.

These discussions constitute the primary observational source from which structural observations are derived.

At this stage, no comparative interpretation is performed.

---

# Stage 2 — Comparative Observation

Each discussion is examined through structural observation.

The objective is not to summarize the discussion, but to identify recurring structural characteristics that can later support comparative analysis.

---

# Stage 3 — Transcript Observation

The results of structural observation are documented as standardized Transcript Observation records.

These Observation records become the primary units of comparative analysis.

Rather than comparing transcripts directly, subsequent analyses compare Observation records.

---

# Stage 4 — Comparative Transcript Observation Corpus

Individual Observation records are accumulated into a Comparative Transcript Observation Corpus.

The Corpus functions as a reusable research resource that preserves observations in a consistent structure.

Additional Observation records may be incorporated without modifying existing observations.

---

# Stage 5 — Comparative Framework

Comparative analysis of the Observation Corpus produces a provisional Comparative Framework.

The Framework synthesizes recurring structural relationships observed across multiple independent discussions.

---

# Stage 6 — Comparative Interpretation

The Comparative Framework supports systematic comparative interpretation.

Interpretation is therefore grounded in accumulated Observation records rather than isolated discussions.

---

# Workflow Characteristics

The proposed workflow exhibits several important characteristics.

- Each stage produces reusable research assets.
- Observation records function as independent comparative units.
- The Observation Corpus supports cumulative comparative research.
- Comparative interpretation emerges from systematically accumulated observations.

---

# Incremental Development

The workflow is designed to support continuous expansion.

```text
New Public Discussion
        │
        ▼
Observation
        │
        ▼
Corpus Expansion
        │
        ▼
Framework Refinement
        │
        ▼
Comparative Interpretation
```

As additional discussions are incorporated, the Observation Corpus expands incrementally while preserving methodological consistency.
