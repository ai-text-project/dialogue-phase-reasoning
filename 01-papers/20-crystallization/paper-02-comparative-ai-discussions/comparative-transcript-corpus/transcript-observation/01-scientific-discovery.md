# Primary Observations

The following structural observations were identified.

- AI is described as accelerating scientific research rather than replacing scientific reasoning.
- Scientific discovery is presented as a collaborative process combining computational capability and human judgment.
- AlphaFold is introduced as an example of AI functioning as scientific research infrastructure.
- Human researchers remain responsible for defining research questions, interpreting results, and determining broader scientific significance.
- Continuous improvement ("Kaizen") is discussed as an operational principle supporting long-duration research development.
