# Comparative AI Discussions

## Overview

This repository contains the research assets associated with the paper:

> **Comparative AI Discussions: A Structural Observation Approach to Public AI Research Dialogues**  
> *(Provisional Title)*

The study proposes a methodology for transforming publicly available AI research discussions into reusable comparative research assets through structural observation.

Rather than comparing transcripts directly, the proposed workflow introduces **Transcript Observation** as the primary unit of comparative analysis.

Individual observations are organized into a **Comparative Transcript Observation Corpus**, from which a **Comparative Framework** is developed through systematic comparative analysis.

---

# Research Workflow

The proposed research workflow is summarized below.

```text
Public AI Research Discussions
        │
        ▼
Comparative Observation
        │
        ▼
Transcript Observation
        │
        ▼
Comparative Transcript Observation Corpus
        │
        ▼
Comparative Framework
        │
        ▼
Comparative Interpretation
```

This workflow enables publicly available discussions to become reusable comparative research assets while preserving structural consistency across independent sources.
