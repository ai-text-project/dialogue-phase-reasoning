## Title

#　A Phase-Structural Model of Intelligence Emergence via Rectification Density and Reflective Pressure:
## A Structural Jump Mapping in Semantic Tensor Space

---

## Abstract

This paper proposes a theoretical model of structural transitions in dialogue-based reasoning. It treats abrupt changes in reasoning behavior not as simple increases in output or performance, but as phase-structural reconfiguration within Semantic Tensor Space. The model is organized around four core components: Rectification Density (R), Reflective Pressure (P), the threshold condition R⋅P≥θ, and the structural transition mapping T→T
′
. Within this framework, structural jump is described as a conceptual representation of how semantic organization may shift from one regime to another when internal structural conditions reach a critical range. The present paper does not offer a standalone measurement protocol, nor does it claim to provide a complete empirical validation procedure. Rather, it contributes a theoretical account of a core structural phenomenon within a broader measurement-compatible research program. In that larger program, extended theoretical foundations and measurement-oriented operationalization are developed elsewhere. The role of this paper is therefore to isolate and formalize one central phenomenon-structure in restrained theoretical terms, while remaining compatible with future empirical operationalization.
