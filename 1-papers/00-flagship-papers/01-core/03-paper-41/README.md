# Paper #41

## Title

**Inner-Core Dominance and the Grokking-Like Jump:**  
*Reorganization of Semantic Structure in Long-Horizon Dialogue*

---

## Overview

This paper explores the possibility that
long-horizon dialogue may reorganize AI output structure
from outer-shell memorization dominance
toward inner-core semantic dominance.

The paper introduces a conceptual three-layer model consisting of:

- Inner Core
- Outer Shell
- Semantic Membrane

and examines how continued dialogue may function
as a form of contextual regularization.

The proposed transition is discussed as a conceptual jump condition
related to grokking-like structural reorganization
within long-horizon dialogue systems.

Rather than functioning as a standalone empirical study,
the paper is positioned as a representative conceptual paper
describing Dialogue-Induced Reorganization.
