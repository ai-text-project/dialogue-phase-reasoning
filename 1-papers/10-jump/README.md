# Stage 1 — Jump

This section collects papers, notes, and related materials associated with **Stage 1: Jump** in the dialogue-phase research program.

Here, *Jump* refers to threshold-like structural reconfiguration: a point at which accumulated pressure, ambiguity, or unresolved structural tension gives rise to a discontinuous shift in configuration rather than mere incremental content growth.

The main concern at this stage is not stabilization or externalization, but the conditions under which structural change becomes observable.

## Main Questions
- When does a structural jump occur?
- What kinds of pressure or rectification conditions precede a jump?
- How can threshold-like reconfiguration be described from observable dialogue outputs?


## Working Perspective
Stage 1 focuses on:
- threshold conditions
- structural reconfiguration
- discontinuity
- observable change
- dialogue-based measurement
- early formalization of jump dynamics

This folder serves as the main entry point for the theoretical side of the research program.
