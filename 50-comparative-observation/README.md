# Purpose

This repository provides a research environment for the systematic observation and comparison of independent theoretical systems.

Rather than attempting to establish conceptual equivalence between different disciplines, this repository investigates whether comparable structural relationships can be identified through comparative observation.

The emphasis is placed on observation before interpretation, and on structural organization before conceptual correspondence.

---

# Research Philosophy

Different theoretical systems often employ different terminology, assumptions, and explanatory frameworks.

Direct comparison at the conceptual level therefore risks introducing premature equivalence.

This repository adopts a different strategy.

Instead of comparing concepts directly, it compares the structural organization underlying each theoretical framework.

Accordingly,

the primary objective is not to determine whether two theories describe the same reality,

but rather to investigate whether comparable organizational relationships can be observed.

---

# Comparative Observation Methodology

The methodology adopted throughout this repository consists of three analytical layers.

## 1. Implementation Layer

This layer concerns implementation-specific mechanisms.

Examples include:

- Latent Space
- Embedding
- Neural Representation
- Algorithmic Components

These elements are specific to particular implementations and are not directly compared across different theoretical systems.

---

## 2. Functional Layer

This layer concerns functional organization.

Typical examples include:

- Representation
- World Model
- Prediction
- Planning
- Projection
- Perception
- Decision

Comparative observation is primarily conducted at this level.

---

## 3. Comparative Layer

The highest analytical layer abstracts from implementation details.

The focus shifts from individual concepts to organizational relationships.

The principal analytical target becomes:

- Internal Organization
- Structural Relationships
- Boundary Transitions
- Functional Topology

At this layer, structural projection becomes possible even when terminology differs substantially.

---

# Observation Policy

The repository follows several fundamental principles.

- Observation precedes interpretation.
- Structural comparison precedes conceptual comparison.
- Similarities and differences are recorded with equal importance.
- Unresolved questions are intentionally preserved.
- No conceptual identity is assumed unless supported by accumulated observations.

---

# Structural Projection

Comparative observation within this repository is performed through Structural Projection.

Rather than matching isolated concepts,

the methodology investigates whether organizational relationships remain comparable across independent theoretical systems.

Accordingly,

comparison focuses upon:

- relationships,
- transformations,
- organizational processes,

rather than terminology alone.
