# World Representation Comparative Research

## Overview

World Representation Comparative Research is an observational research program that investigates how different frameworks internally represent the concept of **World**.

The objective is not to determine which worldview is correct.

Instead, the research seeks to reconstruct each framework independently before conducting structural comparisons.

The program therefore emphasizes **comparative observation** rather than philosophical evaluation.

---

# Research Objective

Many theories discuss concepts such as

* reality,
* perception,
* intelligence,
* prediction,
* action,
* or cognition.

However, these concepts are often examined within the boundaries of a single discipline.

The objective of this research program is different.

It investigates how different frameworks internally organize these concepts into coherent world representations.

Accordingly, the primary unit of comparison is not an isolated concept, but an entire representational structure.

---

# Research Philosophy

The research follows three fundamental principles.

## Independent Reconstruction

Each framework is reconstructed using its own internal concepts before any comparison is performed.

This minimizes interpretative bias and preserves internal consistency.

---

## Structural Observation

Comparisons are conducted at the structural level.

The research focuses on relationships among concepts rather than on doctrinal agreement or disagreement.

---

## Comparative Analysis

Only after independent reconstruction has been completed are different world representations compared.

The comparison therefore concerns representational organization rather than philosophical correctness.
