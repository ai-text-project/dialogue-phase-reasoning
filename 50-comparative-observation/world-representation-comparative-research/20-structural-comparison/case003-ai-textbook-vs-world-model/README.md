# Case003 — AI Textbook vs World Model

**Case Version:** v1.0 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This case investigates whether structural correspondence exists between the World Model described by Demis Hassabis and the structural architecture extracted from the AI Textbook.

The objective is not to compare terminology, chapter organization, or theoretical claims.

Instead, this case focuses on organizational architecture after both research resources have been independently transformed into structural representations.

---

# Comparative Principle

The comparison follows the principle:

**Compare Representations, not Resources.**

Both research resources have already undergone independent structural observation.

Comparative observation therefore operates on their structural representations rather than on their original documents.
