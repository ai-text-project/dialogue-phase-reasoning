# Comparative Analysis

## Purpose

This directory contains the comparative analysis conducted in **Case001**.

The objective of the analysis is **not** to determine which framework is correct.

Instead, the purpose is to observe how two independently reconstructed world representations organize their internal structures.

The comparison therefore focuses on structural organization rather than philosophical evaluation.

---

# Comparative Principle

The comparative analysis follows three principles.

## Independent Reconstruction

Both frameworks are reconstructed independently before any comparison is performed.

Neither framework is interpreted through the conceptual vocabulary of the other.

---

## Structural Comparison

The comparison is performed at the level of structural organization.

Rather than comparing terminology or doctrine, the analysis investigates the functional relationships among core concepts.

---

## Preservation of Internal Consistency

Each framework is evaluated according to its own internal logic.

The objective is not to normalize different worldviews into a single theoretical system.

Instead, each reconstruction preserves the conceptual integrity of the original framework.

# Scope

The analyses contained in this directory should be understood as observational studies.

They do not attempt to establish definitive conclusions regarding either framework.

Instead, they document the structural similarities and differences observed during the first comparative case of the **World Representation Comparative Research** program.
