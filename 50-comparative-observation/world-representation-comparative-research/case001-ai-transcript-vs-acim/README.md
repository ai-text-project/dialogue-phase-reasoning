# Case001 — AI Transcript vs ACIM

## Research Context

This case is part of the **World Representation Comparative Research** program.

Its objective is **not** to compare artificial intelligence and *A Course in Miracles (ACIM)* as general domains.

Instead, the case compares two independently reconstructed **World Representations**.

Accordingly, the comparison should be understood as a comparison between two reconstructed conceptual systems rather than a comparison between AI and ACIM themselves.

## ACIM-side Source

The ACIM-side reconstruction is performed independently from *A Course in Miracles.*

The reconstruction follows the same methodological process used for the AI-side reconstruction.

No comparison is performed until both world representations have first been reconstructed independently.

# Scope

This case does **not** attempt to determine

* which worldview is correct,
* which framework is superior,
* or whether either framework should replace the other.

Instead, the objective is to observe how different frameworks internally organize the concepts of

* World
* Representation
* Prediction
* Action
* Intelligence

through a common comparative perspective.

# Position within the Research Program

Case001 represents the first comparative observation within the **World Representation Comparative Research** series.

Future cases may investigate additional frameworks using the same observational sequence while preserving the principle of independent reconstruction before comparison.

Accordingly, this case should be regarded as an initial observational study rather than a final theoretical conclusion.
