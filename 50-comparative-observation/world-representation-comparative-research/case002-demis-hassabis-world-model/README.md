# Case002 — Demis Hassabis World Model Observation

## Status

Observation Case (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This case investigates the concept of **World Model** as presented by Demis Hassabis in a public AI research lecture.

The objective is not to evaluate whether the AI perspective and the ACIM perspective describe the same reality.

Instead, this case explores whether the two systems preserve comparable structural relationships that can be examined through structural projection.

---

# Background

Case001 compared an AI-generated discussion of world representation with the ACIM framework.

Case002 extends this comparative research by examining the research perspective of Demis Hassabis, whose work emphasizes:

- World Models
- Representation Learning
- Search
- Prediction
- Planning
- Action

This provides an opportunity to compare a contemporary AI research framework with the ACIM framework at the level of structural organization.

---

# Research Question

Can the concept of a World Model in contemporary AI research be structurally compared with the world representation framework described in A Course in Miracles?

If so,

which structural relationships appear to be preserved,

and which remain fundamentally different?

---

# Comparative Method

This case does **not** compare terminology.

Instead,

it compares structural relationships.

The primary comparative categories include:

- World
- Representation
- Search
- Prediction
- Action
- Intelligence

These categories are treated as observational axes rather than predefined equivalences.

---

# Research Policy

The purpose of this case is observation.

No attempt is made to demonstrate conceptual identity between AI research and ACIM.

Observed similarities are recorded.

Observed differences are equally important.

Unresolved issues remain open.
