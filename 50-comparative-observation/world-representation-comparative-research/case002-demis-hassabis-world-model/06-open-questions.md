# Question 1

## What organizes intelligence?

Case002 suggests that intelligent behavior may emerge from coordinated organizational processes rather than from isolated cognitive functions.

However,

the organizing principle itself remains unclear.

Future observations should investigate whether different theoretical systems preserve comparable organizing principles despite employing different conceptual vocabularies.
