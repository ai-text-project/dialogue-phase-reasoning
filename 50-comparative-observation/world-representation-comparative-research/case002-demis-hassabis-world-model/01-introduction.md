# Case002 — Introduction

## Status

Observation Case (Provisional)

---

# Background

As part of the World Representation Comparative Research, this case investigates the concept of the **World Model** presented by Demis Hassabis in a public lecture on artificial intelligence.

Unlike Case001, which analyzed an AI-generated transcript together with the ACIM framework, this case focuses on the research perspective of a leading AI scientist who has contributed to modern AI systems including AlphaGo, AlphaFold, Gemini, and World Model research.

The lecture repeatedly discusses concepts including:

- World Model
- Representation
- Search
- Prediction
- Planning
- Action

These concepts provide a structured description of how modern AI systems internally represent and interact with the external world.

---

# Motivation

During the observation of the lecture transcript, it became apparent that the concept of a **World Model** occupies a central position within the speaker's research philosophy.

At the same time, previous comparative observations within this Research Program have explored how **A Course in Miracles (ACIM)** describes the relationship between mind, world, projection, perception, and experience.

Although these two systems originate from entirely different disciplines, both appear to describe relationships among internal representation, experienced reality, and subsequent behavior.

This observation motivated the initiation of Case002.

---

# Observation Objective

The objective of this case is **not** to determine whether AI research and ACIM describe the same reality.

Instead, this case investigates whether the structural organization of the two systems can be compared through structural projection.

The emphasis is placed on preserving structural relationships rather than identifying conceptual equivalence.

---

# Initial Observation

The lecture suggests that modern AI systems construct internal representations of the world in order to improve prediction, planning, and decision making.

Within the ACIM framework, experience is also described through an organized relationship between mind, perception, projection, and the experienced world.

Whether these two frameworks preserve comparable structural organization remains an open research question.

---

# Comparative Strategy

Rather than comparing terminology directly, this case adopts a layered comparative approach.

The following analytical categories are provisionally selected:

- World
- Representation
- Search
- Prediction
- Action
- Intelligence

These categories function as observational axes.

No assumption is made that corresponding concepts are identical across the two systems.

---

# Position within the Comparative Observation Repository

This case represents the second observation within the World Representation Comparative Research.

Case001 established an initial comparison between AI-generated discussion and ACIM.

Case002 extends the repository by introducing a contemporary AI research framework proposed by Demis Hassabis.

Future cases may continue expanding the repository using the same comparative methodology.

---

# Expected Contribution

This case aims to clarify whether structural projection provides a useful methodology for comparing independent theories of world representation.

Any conclusions will remain provisional until sufficient comparative observations have accumulated across multiple cases.

Accordingly, this document serves as the starting point for a new observational case rather than as a statement of theoretical conclusions.
