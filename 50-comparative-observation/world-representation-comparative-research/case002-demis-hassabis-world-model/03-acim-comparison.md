# Purpose

This document summarizes the framework of world representation described in *A Course in Miracles (ACIM)*.

The objective is observation rather than comparison.

Interpretation and structural comparison are intentionally deferred to later documents within this case.

---

# General Observation

Within ACIM, the experienced world is not presented as an independently existing reality.

Instead, experience is consistently described as emerging through relationships among mind, interpretation, projection, perception, and the experienced world.

The world therefore functions as an experienced consequence of an underlying mental process rather than as an independent starting point.

---

# Observed Structural Components

The following components repeatedly appear throughout the ACIM materials referenced in this case.

- Mind
- Decision
- Ego / Holy Spirit
- Projection
- Perception
- World
- Experience

These components are presented as an interconnected process rather than isolated concepts.

---

# Mind

Mind occupies the central position within the ACIM framework.

Choices are understood as occurring within the mind rather than within the external world.

The mind therefore functions as the origin of subsequent experience.

---

# Projection

Projection is described as a fundamental mechanism through which internal mental content becomes experienced as an external world.

Projection therefore connects internal interpretation with perceived reality.

---

# Perception

Perception is not treated as passive observation.

Instead, perception reflects prior mental interpretation.

The perceived world therefore depends upon preceding mental processes.

---

# World

The world is consistently presented as the experienced outcome of projection and perception.

Rather than functioning as an independent origin,

the world appears as a consequence of mental organization.

---

# Experience

Experience is understood as the result of interactions among mind, projection, perception, and the perceived world.

Experience therefore reflects the overall organization of these processes.

---

# Structural Observation

Across the referenced ACIM materials, the following organizational sequence repeatedly appears.

Mind

↓

Decision

↓

Projection

↓

Perception

↓

Experienced World

Although different texts emphasize different aspects of this process,

the overall organizational structure remains consistent.
