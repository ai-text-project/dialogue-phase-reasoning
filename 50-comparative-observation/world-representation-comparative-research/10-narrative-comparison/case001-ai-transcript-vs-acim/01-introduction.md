# Introduction

## Purpose

This case study investigates how different frameworks internally represent **World**.

Rather than evaluating which framework is correct, the objective is to reconstruct each worldview independently and then compare their structural characteristics.

The present case focuses on two frameworks:

* An AI-oriented worldview reconstructed from a scientific AI transcript discussing World Models, Representation, Prediction, and Action.
* The worldview presented in *A Course in Miracles (ACIM).*

The comparison is therefore conducted at the level of **World Representation**, not at the level of philosophy, theology, or artificial intelligence.

---

# Research Background

The study began with the analysis of a scientific AI transcript discussing the relationship between World Models and Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI).

During the observation process, it became apparent that the transcript was not merely discussing AI architectures.

Instead, it implicitly assumed a particular way of representing the world.

This shifted the focus of the research from AI technology itself to a broader question:

> How does a framework internally represent "World"?

As a result, the research expanded into a comparative study of world representations.

---

# Research Objective

The purpose of this case is to reconstruct and compare the internal structures of two independent world representations.

The comparison is performed through a common set of structural concepts rather than through doctrinal or historical analysis.

The primary observation targets include:

* World
* Representation
* Observation
* Prediction
* Action
* Intelligence

Each framework is first reconstructed independently.

Only after both reconstructions are completed are they compared.

---

# Research Policy

This case follows the following research sequence:

```text
Observation

↓

Internal Structural Reconstruction

↓

Representation Mapping

↓

Comparative Analysis

↓

Discussion

↓

Open Questions
```

This order is maintained throughout the study to minimize interpretative bias.

Neither framework is evaluated before its own internal structure has been reconstructed.

---

# Scope of This Case

This document is not intended to determine whether either worldview is true.

Instead, it documents one comparative observation within the broader **World Representation Comparative Research** program.

Accordingly, the emphasis is placed on structural relationships rather than philosophical conclusions.

This case therefore serves as the first observational record in an ongoing series of comparative analyses involving different representations of reality.

---

# Position within the Research Program

This document constitutes **Case001** of the World Representation Comparative Research series.

Its role is to establish the initial comparative framework between an AI-oriented world representation and the worldview presented in ACIM.

Future cases may extend the same observational methodology to additional frameworks while preserving the same comparative perspective.
