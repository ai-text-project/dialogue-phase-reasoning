# Future Observations

## Purpose

This chapter outlines potential directions for future observation arising from the present case.

The objective is not to predict future conclusions.

Instead, it identifies promising areas in which the comparative investigation of world representations may be extended.

Accordingly, this chapter serves as a bridge between Case001 and future comparative studies.

---

# Observation 1

## Expansion to Additional World Representations

The present case compares two independently reconstructed world representations.

Future studies may extend the same observational sequence to additional frameworks.

Potential candidates include:

* Active Inference
* Predictive Processing
* Buddhist philosophical traditions
* Phenomenology
* Other cognitive or philosophical frameworks

The purpose is not to establish a universal theory, but to observe how different systems internally organize the concepts of World, Representation, Prediction, Action, and Intelligence.
