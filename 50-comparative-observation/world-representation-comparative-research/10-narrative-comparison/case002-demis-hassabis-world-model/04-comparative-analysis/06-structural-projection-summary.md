# Summary of Comparative Observations

## World and Representation

Both frameworks distinguish between an internally organized domain and an experienced world.

However,

the direction of organization differs.

The AI framework emphasizes internal representation of the external world.

The ACIM framework emphasizes projection from the internal mind toward experienced reality.

---

## Representation and Search

Within the AI framework,

representation supports efficient search.

Within ACIM,

organized mental processes guide interpretation, projection, and perception.

Although the organizational mechanisms differ,

both frameworks preserve structured internal processes preceding experience.

---

## Prediction and Time

The AI framework organizes future action through prediction.

The ACIM framework organizes future experience through temporal projection.

The organizational purpose differs,

yet both frameworks require internally organized anticipation of future states.

---

## Action and Projection

Within the AI framework,

internal organization culminates in adaptive action.

Within ACIM,

internal organization culminates in projected experience.

The mechanisms differ,

yet both describe structured transitions from internal organization toward external consequences.

---

## Organizing Principle

Neither framework attributes organization to a single isolated component.

Instead,

both describe coordinated systems composed of multiple interacting processes.

Accordingly,

the comparison suggests that organization itself may represent a meaningful comparative category.
