# Purpose

This document examines the relationship between **World** and **Representation** within two independent theoretical frameworks.

The first framework is the World Model described by Demis Hassabis.

The second framework is the world representation described in *A Course in Miracles (ACIM)*.

The objective is not to establish conceptual equivalence.

Instead, this document investigates whether comparable structural relationships are preserved.

---

# AI Observation

Within the lecture, the external world functions as the environment that an intelligent system must understand.

Rather than responding directly to raw observations,

the system constructs an internal representation.

This representation supports prediction, planning, and intelligent action.

Accordingly,

the observed relationship may be summarized as:

World

↓

Internal Representation

---

# ACIM Observation

Within ACIM,

the experienced world is not treated as the starting point.

Instead,

the mind gives rise to projection,

projection influences perception,

and perception gives rise to the experienced world.

Accordingly,

the observed relationship may be summarized as:

Mind

↓

Projection

↓

Perceived World

---

# Initial Structural Observation

Although the two systems describe different domains,

both distinguish between:

- an experienced world,
- an internal organizing process,
- and a relationship connecting the two.

At the present observational stage,

the nature of that relationship differs substantially.

Within the AI framework,

representation appears to reconstruct aspects of the external world.

Within ACIM,

projection participates in constructing the experienced world itself.

---

# Structural Projection

The present comparison does not assume that

Representation

and

Projection

describe identical processes.

Instead,

the observation concerns the structural relationship between

an internal organizational process

and

an experienced world.

At this level,

both systems appear to distinguish:

External or Experienced World

↓

Internal Organization

↓

Subsequent Cognitive Process

The direction and interpretation of these relationships remain different.

---

# Observed Difference

One important difference becomes immediately visible.

The AI framework primarily models the transition:

World

↓

Representation

In contrast,

ACIM primarily describes:

Mind

↓

Projection

↓

Perceived World

Accordingly,

the direction of structural organization appears to differ.

The AI framework emphasizes reconstruction.

The ACIM framework emphasizes projection.

---

# Preliminary Observation

an internal organizational domain

an experienced world.

However,

the causal direction relating these domains appears fundamentally different.

Whether these differences nevertheless preserve deeper structural relationships remains an open question.
