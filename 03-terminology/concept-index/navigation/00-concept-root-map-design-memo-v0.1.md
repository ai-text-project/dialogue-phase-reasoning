# Concept Root Map Design Memo v0.1

## Purpose

This memo defines the design rationale for the Concept Root Map.

The objective is not to establish a finalized theoretical model,
but to provide an intermediate navigation structure connecting:

* Concept Index
* Concept Graph
* Root Concepts
* Theory Skeleton

within the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning research framework.

---

## Background

The current repository contains:

* Concept Index resources
* Concept relationship mappings
* Conceptual navigation structures
* Theory-oriented research papers

While individual concepts and relationships have been organized,
the structural pathway from concepts to theoretical organization
remains distributed across multiple documents.

The Concept Root Map is intended to provide a lightweight bridge
between conceptual resources and higher-level theoretical structures.

---

## Current Inputs

The current design is based on:

* Core6 Concept Index Table
* Related Concept Index structures
* Concept Graph exploration materials
* Concept Index v0.8 exploratory mappings

These resources are treated as inputs rather than finalized conclusions.

---

## Design Assumptions

The current working hypothesis assumes that:

1. Concepts may form graph-like structures.
2. Certain concepts may function as organizational roots.
3. Root concepts may provide access to higher-level theoretical skeletons.
4. The resulting structure remains provisional and subject to revision.

---

## Candidate Root Concepts

Current exploratory candidates include:

* Projection
* Resonance
* Phase

These candidates are treated as organizational anchors only.

Their theoretical status remains under investigation.
