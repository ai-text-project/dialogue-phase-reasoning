# Research Program Protection Policy v1

## Purpose

To preserve the integrity, continuity, and independence of the core research program while allowing multiple external-facing initiatives to develop in parallel.

---

## Core Principle

The Research Program is the primary system.

All external-facing activities exist to support, sustain, or connect the research program.

They do not determine its direction.

---

## Governance Rule 01

### Research First

The purpose of external initiatives is not to redefine the research program.

External initiatives may observe, support, interpret, translate, or connect research outputs.

They may not dictate theoretical direction.

---

## Governance Rule 02

### No Reverse Control

Employment activities,

Harvest activities,

Community activities,

must not directly alter:

- theoretical structures
- case classifications
- research mapping decisions
- observation criteria

without independent review.

---

## Governance Rule 03

### Role Separation

Research Program

↓

Cartographer

↓

Curator

↓

Harvest

↓

Career

↓

Community

Each role operates within its own scope.

Cross-role recommendations are advisory only.

---

## Governance Rule 04

### Advisory Status

All recommendations generated by:

- Harvest Strategist
- Harvest Curator
- Research-to-Career Strategist
- Research Community Mapping

shall be treated as advisory observations.

Implementation decisions remain outside their authority.

---

## Governance Rule 05

### Preservation of Research Identity

Research shall not be modified solely because:

- it is more employable
- it is more marketable
- it is more popular
- it attracts more attention

Research direction remains observation-centered.

---

## Governance Rule 06

### Parallel Exploration

Multiple external routes may be explored simultaneously.

Examples:

- Employment Route
- Harvest Route
- Community Route

Exploration does not imply commitment.

---

## Governance Rule 07

### Research Program Independence

The research program remains a distinct and independent system.

External routes are satellites.

The research program is the center.
