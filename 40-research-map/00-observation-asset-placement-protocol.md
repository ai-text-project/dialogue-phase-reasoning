# Observation Asset Placement Protocol

---

## Status

**Operational Protocol**

**Research Mapping Asset**

Version: v0.1

---

## Purpose

This document records the current operational protocol used when determining the placement of newly created observation assets.

The purpose is not to define theoretical classifications.

Instead, the purpose is to provide a repeatable placement procedure based on the primary object of observation.

---

## Core Principle

The placement decision should be based on:

> **What is being observed?**

rather than:

* topic similarity
* terminology overlap
* repository proximity

The primary object of observation determines the destination.

## Placement Protocol

### Step 1 — New Observation Asset

A new observation asset is generated.

Examples:

* Case
* Research Note
* Open Question
* Observation Memo
* Governance Observation
* Operational Observation

---

### Step 2 — Initial Placement Question

Ask:

> Where should this be placed?

Do not immediately classify by topic.

Proceed to identify the primary object of observation.

---

### Step 3 — Identify Primary Observation Object

Ask:

> What is being observed?

Determine the dominant observation focus.

Typical categories include:

* Structure
* Research Question
* Observation Process
* Program Evolution

---

### Step 4 — Determine Placement Destination

#### Structure

Examples:

* Structural stabilization
* Structural clarification
* Crystallization observations

Destination:

```text
20-crystallization/
```

---

#### Research Question

Examples:

* Open-question formation
* Comparative exploration
* Research interpretation

Destinations:

```text
40-research-map/
├─ 05-research-notes/
└─ 02-open-questions/
```

---

#### Observation Process

Examples:

* Recursive observation
* Observation-method observations
* Classification-related observations

Destination:

```text
40-research-map/
└─ 00-emergence-observations/
```

---

#### Program Evolution

Examples:

* Governance evolution
* Operational continuity
* Role stabilization
* Research-program development

Destination:

```text
ai-text-project-hub/

└─ 0-emergence-notes/
   └─ 00-research-program-evolution/
```

---

### Step 5 — Placement Decision

Select the destination corresponding to the primary observation object.

When multiple interpretations are possible:

* identify the dominant observation focus
* record secondary interpretations if necessary

---

### Step 6 — Repository Recording

Record the asset in the selected destination.

If classification remains uncertain:

```text
40-research-map/
└─ 00-emergence-observations/
```

may be used as a provisional holding area until further clarification becomes possible.
