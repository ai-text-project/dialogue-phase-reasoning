## Status

Open research framework for observational comparison.

This document does NOT define Crystallization conclusively.

Instead, it proposes tentative observational criteria for comparing Case structures related to:

* stabilization
* semantic persistence
* recursive reuse
* low-loss projection
* operational continuity

within the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning research program.

---

# Core Research Direction

Current research does not assume that Crystallization should be defined a priori.

Instead, the working hypothesis is:

> Crystallization may become progressively definable through repeated comparison of observable Case structures.

The primary research direction is therefore:

Case observation
↓
comparative analysis
↓
pattern extraction
↓
conditional stabilization analysis
↓
tentative definition refinement

---

# Preliminary Working Definition

Tentatively:

> Crystallization refers to a stabilized state in which semantically rectified structures remain reusable across time, role separation, and external form transformation with relatively low reconstruction cost.

This definition remains provisional and subject to revision through future Case comparison.

---

# Proposed Observation Criteria

The following criteria are proposed as lightweight observational indicators.

These are not binary conditions, but comparative dimensions.

---

## 1. Retention Stability

Question:

* Does the observed structure remain stable over time?

Observation examples:

* retained topology
* recurring semantic continuity
* persistent organizational coherence

---

## 2. Reusability

Question:

* Can the same structure be reused across:

  * different tasks
  * different documents
  * different Roles
  * different external forms

Observation examples:

* repeated conceptual reuse
* transferable organizational structure
* cross-domain applicability

---

## 3. Low Reconstruction Cost

Question:

* Can the structure be reactivated without rebuilding from zero?

Observation examples:

* lightweight continuation
* reduced setup burden
* stable re-entry into prior semantic organization

---

## 4. Compression Resistance

Question:

* Does the semantic core survive density reduction?

Observation examples:

* Detailed → Simple transitions
* AI Textbook → Medium / Mini transformations
* summary compression with preserved structure

---

## 5. Structure-Preserving Externalization

Question:

* Can the structure be projected into external forms with limited semantic loss?

Observation examples:

* GitHub topology projection
* README-layer stabilization
* figure transformation
* repository organization

---

## 6. Boundary Stability

Question:

* Are role, terminology, and structural boundaries preserved without semantic collapse?

Observation examples:

* stable role separation
* governance persistence
* glossary / taxonomy / concept-index consistency
* prevention of topology inflation

---

## 7. Repeatability

Question:

* Does similar stabilization recur under related conditions?

Observation examples:

* repeated structural emergence
* recurring stabilization patterns
* reproducible topology preservation

---

# Proposed Lightweight Evaluation Method

At the current stage, a lightweight observational scoring model may be sufficient.

Example:

| Score | Meaning             |
| ----- | ------------------- |
| 0     | not observed        |
| 1     | weakly observed     |
| 2     | clearly observed    |
| 3     | repeatedly observed |

This scoring model is tentative and intended primarily for comparative Case observation.

# Recommended Comparative Axes

Potential comparison conditions include:

* existing thread vs new thread
* initialized vs non-initialized interaction
* role separation present vs absent
* anti-expansion constraints present vs absent
* projection-oriented vs migration-oriented handling
* lightweight topology vs inflated topology

These comparisons may help clarify stabilization-supporting conditions.

---

# Important Methodological Principle

The current approach prioritizes:

> “what remains stable”

rather than:

> “what appears impressive”

The primary observational target is therefore:

* retained structure
* reusable organization
* persistent semantic continuity

rather than isolated output quality.

---

# Tentative Research Position

At the present stage, Crystallization is treated not as:

* a finalized theory
* a fixed ontological claim

but rather as:

* a developing observational category
* a comparative stabilization hypothesis
* a research-space persistence phenomenon

subject to continued refinement through future Case accumulation.

---

# Open Questions

Several major uncertainties remain open:

* Which criteria are most essential?
* Which criteria are secondary effects?
* How much repeatability is necessary?
* How much cross-domain transfer is required?
* Can stabilization occur without externalization?
* Can externalization itself reinforce stabilization?
* What operational conditions most strongly support persistence?

These questions remain active research topics.
