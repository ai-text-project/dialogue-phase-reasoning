# Purpose

This document provides a provisional structural projection of the relationships observed within `40-research-map`.

The purpose of this map is not to redesign the existing folder structure.

Instead, it externalizes relationships that are already present across the Research Map so that the domain can be understood as a connected research environment rather than as a collection of independent folders and documents.

The existing repository structure remains unchanged.

---

# Structural Principle

The Research Map does not appear to form a simple hierarchy.

Its internal relationships include at least four different structural types:

1. **Developmental Relationships**
2. **Transformational Relationships**
3. **Connective Relationships**
4. **Recursive Relationships**

These relationships operate simultaneously.

The structural map therefore represents a research environment in which observation, methodological formation, operational projection, and structural reflection can influence one another.
