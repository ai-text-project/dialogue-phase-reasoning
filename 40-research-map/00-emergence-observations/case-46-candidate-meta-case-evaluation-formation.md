## Observation

### Observation 1

Cluster Review activities did not terminate after:

* Cluster D identification
* Cluster E identification
* D-E relationship analysis

Instead, the review process continued into evaluation of candidate interpretations.

---

### Observation 2

Candidate Meta Case 01D became an explicit evaluation target.

Discussion shifted from:

* candidate collection

toward:

* candidate assessment
* interpretation comparison
* explanatory evaluation

---

### Observation 3

Multiple interpretations were organized and comparatively assessed.

Examples included:

* Structural Homology
* Research-Space Evolution Revisited
* Topology as Cognitive Style
* additional candidate explanations

The discussion focused on relationships among interpretations rather than creation of new concepts.

---

### Observation 4

A provisional distinction emerged between:

* core explanations
* peripheral explanations

This represented a higher level of organizational activity than simple candidate accumulation.

---

### Observation 5

Topology as Cognitive Style emerged as a possible explanation of the recognition process itself rather than the observed phenomenon.

This shifted part of the discussion toward evaluation of explanatory mechanisms.

---

### Observation 6

Evaluation activities generated additional organizational structures.

These included:

* interpretation hierarchy
* assessment pathways
* explanatory categorization

The evaluation process itself became observable.
