## ⑤ Self-Observation Commentary

The most notable feature was not the emergence of a new theory.

Instead, attention shifted toward the mechanism by which interpretations were repeatedly stabilized and re-stabilized.

### Potentially Related Phenomena

* Recursive crystallization
* Reflective stabilization
* Cumulative reinterpretation
* Governance-constrained refinement
* Observation of observation processes

The proposed spiral metaphor appears notable.

The dialogue repeatedly revisited similar themes while increasing observational resolution.

It remains unclear whether this represents:

* A higher-order form of Crystallization
* A Meta Observation process
* A distinct observation category

Classification should therefore remain provisional.
