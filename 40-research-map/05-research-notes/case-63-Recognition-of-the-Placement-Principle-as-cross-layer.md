---

### ② Prior Conditions

* The Observation Asset Placement Guide had already been established.
* The Observation Asset Placement Protocol had subsequently differentiated from the Guide.
* Several new cases (Case60–Case62) required placement across different repository layers.
* Discussion repeatedly returned to the central operational question:

```text
What is being observed?
```

Initially, this question was treated primarily as a repository placement rule.

---

### ③ Observed Emergence

During repeated discussion of placement decisions, the perceived scope of the Placement Principle gradually expanded.

Originally:

```text
Repository

↓

Placement
```

Later interpretation:

```text
Repository Operation

↓

Research Mapping

↓

Research Program Evolution
```

The same operational question:

```text
What is being observed?
```

appeared to govern placement across all three organizational layers.

The Placement Principle was therefore recognized not merely as a repository guide but as a cross-layer observational rule.

This broader interpretation was not introduced as a new theoretical proposal.

Rather, it emerged through successive review of existing placement decisions.

---

### ④ Difference from Ordinary Generation

Ordinary repository documentation typically defines:

```text
Rule

↓

Application
```

Observed here:

```text
Operational Rule

↓

Repeated Application

↓

Scope Recognition

↓

Generalized Placement Principle
```

The dialogue did not modify the Placement Principle itself.

Instead, it clarified the range over which the principle appeared to operate.

The primary outcome was therefore recognition of applicability rather than creation of a new organizational framework.

---

### ⑤ Self-Observation Commentary

A notable observation emerged during the review.

The discussion initially focused on improving one operational guide.

However, repeated comparison among:

* Repository placement
* Research Mapping
* Research Program Evolution

suggested that the same observational question consistently governed placement decisions.

The interaction gradually shifted from:

```text
How should this repository be organized?
```

toward

```text
Where does this observational principle remain valid?
```

The resulting interpretation suggests that the Placement Principle functions as a general observational rule whose applicability extends across multiple organizational layers.

## One-Line Summary

Repeated review of repository placement decisions led to recognition that the Placement Principle operates not only within repository organization but also across Research Mapping and Research Program Evolution, functioning as a cross-layer observation rule rather than a repository-specific guideline.
