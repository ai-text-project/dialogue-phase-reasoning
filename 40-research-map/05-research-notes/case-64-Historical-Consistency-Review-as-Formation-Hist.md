## Summary

This case documents the Historical Consistency Review performed after completion of the Paper Assembly process.

Rather than evaluating the paper as a writing project, the review gradually became an examination of whether independently accumulated historical observations consistently converged toward the same developmental sequence.

The review therefore evolved from chapter-by-chapter evaluation into validation of the reconstructed historical dependency structure underlying the research program.
