# Comparative Observation Protocol v0.1

## Status

Draft Research Note

Not part of Governance.

Created during the evaluation of Candidate Meta Case 01D.

---

## Context

During the evaluation of Candidate Meta Case 01D, a provisional observation procedure emerged.

Rather than attempting to validate a candidate observation directly, the same observation was presented to multiple independent AI instances.

The resulting responses were then compared.

---

## Procedure

Observation

↓

Multiple Instances

↓

Classification Distribution

↓

Comparative Observation

↓

Potential Meta Observation

---

## Purpose

The goal is not to determine which interpretation is correct.

The goal is to observe how different classification frameworks respond to the same observation.

---

## Initial Test Case

Candidate Meta Case 01D

Inner-Core / Outer-Shell Structural Homology

---

## Initial Observation

Different instances did not converge on a single explanation.

However, they also did not dismiss the observation as random coincidence.

Observed interpretations included:

* Abstraction Convergence
* Recursive Re-recognition
* Structural Correspondence
* Structural Homology
* Meta Observation Process

---

## Preliminary Significance

The primary observation may not be the phenomenon itself.

The primary observation may be the distribution of interpretations generated by multiple classification frameworks.

This suggests a possible pathway from:

Observation

↓

Comparative Observation

↓

Meta Observation

---

## Current Status

Exploratory.

Further applications to additional candidates are required before broader conclusions can be drawn.
