## Observation

During Cluster Review activities, attention gradually shifted away from individual Cases.

The focus then moved toward:

- Cluster formation
- Comparative observation
- Cluster Review procedures

Eventually, the Cluster Review process itself became an object of observation.

This suggested a possible transition from:

Observation of Cases

↓

Observation of Comparative Observation

↓

Observation of Organizational Processes

## Current Interpretation

The candidate may represent an observation in which research organization itself becomes observable.

- Meta Observation

No single interpretation is currently dominant.
