# Research Note

## Title

Asset Layer Community Alignment Question

---

## Purpose

This note records a question that emerged during Community Mapping and Asset-to-Community Mapping activities.

The purpose is not to answer the question.

The purpose is to preserve an observation regarding possible relationships between research assets and external research communities.

---

## Background

The observation emerged during:

* Community Mapping
* Asset-to-Community Mapping
* Participation Pathway Analysis
* Research Ecosystem Exploration

The original assumption was that a research program would primarily align with a single research community.

However, repeated mapping activities suggested a different possibility.

---

## Observed Shift

Initial assumption:

Research Program

↓

Research Community

Later observation:

Research Program

↓

Asset Inventory

↓

Asset Layers

↓

Multiple Community Cultures

The discussion suggested that different layers of research assets may align with different research communities rather than converging upon a single community.
