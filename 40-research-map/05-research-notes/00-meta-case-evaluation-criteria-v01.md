# Meta Case Evaluation Criteria v0.1

## Status

Draft Research Note

Not part of Governance.

Subject to revision based on future Meta Case evaluations.

---

## Purpose

This document provides provisional criteria for evaluating Meta Case candidates within the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning research program.

The purpose is not to determine truth or theoretical validity.

The purpose is to support consistent placement decisions within the Research Mapping layer.

---

## Scope

Applicable to:

* Candidate Meta Cases
* Proposed Meta Cases
* Comparative observations considered for Meta Case promotion

Not applicable to:

* Individual Cases
* Open Questions
* Interpretation Notes
* Research-Space Evolution observations

---

## Core Principle

A Meta Case should represent an observed pattern that extends beyond a single Case and contributes to the organization of the Research Map.

Meta Cases should not be created solely because an observation appears interesting, novel, or theoretically appealing.

Promotion requires evidence of broader structural significance.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

## Criterion A: Cross-Case Scope

Question:

Does the observation extend beyond a single Case?

Indicators:

* Appears across multiple Cases
* Connects previously separate observations
* Supports comparative analysis

---

## Criterion B: Structural Recurrence

Question:

Has a similar structural pattern appeared more than once?

Indicators:

* Repeated emergence
* Consistent structural relationships
* Recurrence across contexts

---

## Criterion C: Explanatory Function

Question:

Does the observation help explain other Cases or observations?

Indicators:

* Clarifies relationships
* Reduces ambiguity
* Provides organizing value

---

## Criterion D: Research Mapping Impact

Question:

Does the observation affect the structure or interpretation of the Research Map?

Indicators:

* Influences placement decisions
* Changes relationships between observations
* Reveals higher-order organization

---

# Provisional Placement Guide

## Meta Case

Typical condition:

* Satisfies most criteria
* Demonstrates stable cross-case significance
* Contributes to Research Mapping

---

## Candidate Meta Case

Typical condition:

* Promising pattern observed
* Evidence remains limited
* Additional observation required

---

## Interpretation Note

Typical condition:

* Primarily interpretive
* Limited observational support
* Functions as a possible explanation

---

## Open Question

Typical condition:

* Centers on unresolved uncertainty
* Observation remains insufficient
* Further investigation required

---

# Evaluation Philosophy

The burden of promotion should be higher than the burden of retention.

When uncertainty exists, retain Candidate status until sufficient evidence accumulates.

The goal is stability of classification rather than rapid expansion of Meta Cases.
