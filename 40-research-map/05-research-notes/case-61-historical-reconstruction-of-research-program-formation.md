## Summary

This observation records a historical reconstruction process conducted across multiple repositories, formation-history documents, topology records, and paper skeletons.

The primary activity was not theory generation.

Instead, existing research assets were reviewed, compared, and reconnected in order to clarify the developmental relationships among them.

The resulting reconstruction suggested that several previously separate narratives may represent interconnected developmental streams within a larger research-program formation process.

The resulting model may be regarded as a provisional formation-history reconstruction of the research program.
