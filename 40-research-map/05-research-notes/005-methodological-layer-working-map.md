## Purpose

This document provides a provisional organizational map of the Methodological Layer currently emerging within the research program.

Its purpose is not to establish a permanent taxonomy.

Instead, it summarizes the present interpretation derived from accumulated Meta Observations concerning the development of structural observation methodology.

The map is intended to support future observation, comparison, and refinement of the Methodological Layer as additional observations become available.

---

## Background

Recent Meta Cases have progressively shifted attention from individual methodological components toward the organization of the Methodological Layer itself.

Initially, observations focused on the emergence of individual methodological artifacts.

Subsequent observations increasingly examined:

- relationships among methodological components,
- the observer's methodological position,
- methodological refinement processes,
- and the operational roles responsible for maintaining methodological consistency.

Together these observations suggest that the Methodological Layer itself has become an observable organizational structure.

## Working Perspective

The present map should not be interpreted as a fixed taxonomy.

Instead, it represents a provisional organizational interpretation of how different methodological observations currently appear to relate to one another.

Each dimension has emerged through repeated observation rather than prior theoretical design.

Future observations may refine, expand, reorganize, or even replace the present interpretation.

---

## Interpretation

### Structural

Focuses on the organization of methodology itself.

Typical observations include:

- methodological architecture
- framework formation
- relationships among methodological documents
- repository-supported methodology

Representative observation:

- Case 81

---

### Positional

Focuses on the methodological position adopted during structural observation.

Typical observations include:

- observer positioning
- methodological self-observation
- clarification of observational perspective
- observation standpoint

Representative observation:

- Case 84

---

### Procedural

Focuses on the processes through which methodology gradually becomes stabilized.

Typical observations include:

- rectification
- comparative consistency evaluation
- conceptual role refinement
- methodological stabilization

Representative observation:

- Case 85

---

### Operational

Focuses on the operational roles through which methodology is organized and maintained.

Typical observations include:

- role specialization
- methodological architecture mapping
- repository interpretation
- operational methodological organization

Representative observation:

- Case 86

## Relationship among the Four Dimensions

The four dimensions should not be interpreted as independent categories.

Instead, they appear to participate in a continuous developmental cycle.

```text
Methodological Architecture
        │
        ▼
Observer Position
        │
        ▼
Methodological Process
        │
        ▼
Operational Role
        │
        ▼
Refined Methodological Architecture
```

Repeated methodological observation may therefore generate recursive refinement rather than linear progression.

---

## Current Interpretation

Current observations suggest that Methodological Layer development involves simultaneous refinement of:

- methodological structure,
- observational position,
- methodological process,
- operational role.

Rather than developing independently, these dimensions appear to co-evolve through repeated structural observation.

This interpretation remains provisional.
