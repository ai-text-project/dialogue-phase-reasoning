# Interpretation Note

## Research-Space Evolution Revisited

Recent observations suggest that the concept of Research-Space Evolution may describe not only changes in repository structure or research organization.

Through repeated observation, mapping, and externalization, the evolution of the research space increasingly appears coupled with changes in the observer's own cognitive organization.

Under this interpretation:

Research-Space Evolution

may be understood provisionally as:

Research-Space Development
↔
Cognitive Reorganization

This interpretation does not replace the original architectural model.

Instead, it provides an observational example that makes the Level 5 concept more concrete.

Status: Provisional interpretation requiring further observation.
