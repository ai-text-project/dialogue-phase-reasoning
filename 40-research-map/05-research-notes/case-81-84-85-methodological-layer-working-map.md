## Purpose

This document provides a provisional map of the internal organization currently emerging within the Methodological Layer.

Its purpose is not to establish a fixed taxonomy.

Instead, it records the present interpretation of how multiple methodological observations appear to relate to one another across recent Meta Cases.

The map is intended to support future observation, comparison, and methodological refinement.

---

## Background

Recent Meta Cases increasingly shifted attention from individual observations toward the methodology used to perform observation itself.

Rather than describing isolated methodological components, the discussions gradually revealed several distinct but related dimensions of methodological development.

This document records the current working interpretation of those dimensions.

## Interpretation

### Structural

Focuses on the organization of the methodology itself.

Typical observations include:

- methodological architecture
- framework formation
- relationships among methodological documents
- repository-supported organization

Representative observation:

- Case 81

---

### Positional

Focuses on the position adopted by the observer during methodological development.

Typical observations include:

- observer positioning
- methodological self-observation
- clarification of observational perspective

Representative observation:

- Case 84

---

### Procedural

Focuses on how methodology gradually becomes stabilized through repeated observation.

Typical observations include:

- rectification
- comparative consistency evaluation
- refinement of conceptual roles
- stabilization through repeated comparison

Representative observation:

- Case 85

---

## Relationship among the Three Dimensions

The three dimensions should not be regarded as independent categories.

Instead, they appear to interact continuously.

```text
Methodological Architecture
        │
        ▼
Observer Position
        │
        ▼
Methodological Process
        │
        ▼
Refined Methodological Architecture
```

Repeated methodological observation may therefore produce a recursive cycle of refinement rather than a linear sequence of development.
