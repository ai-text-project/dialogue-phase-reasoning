# Scope Expansion

This document tracks observations related to scope expansion within the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning research space.

The focus is not on simple scale increase, but on how semantic mediation layers gradually extend across repositories, navigation structures, and explanatory interfaces.

Observed tendencies include:

- expansion of navigation layers
- cross-repository mediation
- topology-aware interpretation support
- emergence of multi-layer research interfaces
- transition from storage structure to semantic traversal structure

Key questions include:

- At what point does scope expansion alter interpretation behavior?
- Can topology expansion produce new mediation layers?
- How does navigation density influence explanatory behavior?
- Does repository-scale organization affect semantic stabilization?

This document remains exploratory and observational.
