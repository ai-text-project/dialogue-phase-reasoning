# Cluster Review Protocol v0.1

## Purpose

This document records the working procedure used for Cluster Review activities within the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning research space.

The objective of Cluster Review is not theory formation.

Instead, the objective is to identify recurring structural patterns, provisional cluster candidates, and open questions emerging from comparative observation of accumulated Cases.

---

## Input Sources

Cluster Review may use:

* Case Records
* Selected Cases
* Meta Cases
* Open Questions
* Related Research Notes

Cases are treated as observational records rather than theoretical conclusions.

---

## Review Procedure

### Step 1 — Case Collection

Identify the Cases to be included within the review scope.

Possible scopes include:

* consecutive Cases
* Selected Cases
* Cases belonging to a specific topic area
* Meta Cases

The review scope should be explicitly recorded.

---

### Step 2 — Comparative Observation

Compare Cases to identify:

* recurring structures
* recurring sequences
* recurring relationships
* recurring organizational patterns

The focus is on similarities and differences between Cases.

---

### Step 3 — Cluster Candidate Formation

Group Cases according to observed structural similarities.

Clusters are provisional and subject to revision.

A Case may contribute to more than one Cluster when justified by observation.

---

### Step 4 — Cluster Characterization

For each Cluster, record:

* defining characteristics
* representative Cases
* observed variations
* unresolved ambiguities

No causal claims are required.

---

### Step 5 — Open Question Extraction

Extract recurring questions emerging from the comparison process.

Examples include:

* cluster boundaries
* cluster relationships
* missing observations
* possible future observations

Open Questions remain exploratory.

---

### Step 6 — Cross-Cluster Observation

Compare Clusters with one another.

Possible observations include:

* hierarchy
* sequence
* dependency
* overlap
* transition patterns

The objective is to identify higher-order relationships.

---

### Step 7 — Meta Observation (Optional)

If observation shifts from:

```text
Case
↓
Cluster
↓
Observation Process
```

record the result as a Meta Observation candidate.

This step is optional and applies only when the observation process itself becomes the object of observation.

---

## Outputs

Typical outputs include:

* Cluster Review reports
* Open Questions
* Cross-Cluster observations
* Meta Case candidates
* Research Mapping candidates

---

## Non-Goals

Cluster Review does not attempt to:

* prove theories
* establish causality
* perform statistical validation
* finalize classifications

All outputs remain provisional and observational.
