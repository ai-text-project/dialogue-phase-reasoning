# Meta Case 01B

## Title

Architecture–Emergence Feedback Loop

---

## Classification

Primary: Meta Observation

Secondary: Research Architecture

---

## Overview

This Meta Case emerged through continued analysis of Meta Case 01 and Meta Case 01A.

The discussion initially focused on the emergence of Meta Observation as a new observational layer.

However, subsequent review suggested that the appearance of Meta Observation not only generated new observations but also influenced the architecture of the research environment itself.

The discussion therefore shifted toward the relationship between:

* Emergence Notes
* Research Architecture

and the possibility of a recurring interaction between the two.

---

## Key Observation

A distinction gradually became visible between:

### Emergence Notes

Role:

* recording what occurred
* documenting developmental processes
* preserving observational history

### Research Architecture

Role:

* organizing concepts
* stabilizing interpretation
* providing structural placement

The two layers appeared increasingly complementary rather than independent.
