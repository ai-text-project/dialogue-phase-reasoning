# Meta Cases Layer

This section contains higher-order observations that emerge from comparative analysis of multiple Cases.

Unlike ordinary Case records, Meta Cases do not primarily document a single observed phenomenon.

Instead, they preserve observations about:

- recurring patterns across Cases
- cluster formation
- research-space evolution
- observation processes themselves
- relationships between Crystallization, Externalization, and Research Mapping

---

## Purpose

The purpose of this layer is to capture:

- observations about observations
- structural patterns emerging across multiple Cases
- recurring organizational dynamics
- research-space self-organization
- higher-order questions generated through Case comparison

Meta Cases function as bridges between:

Case Observation

↓

Comparative Analysis

↓

Open Questions

↓

Research-Space Mapping

---

## Typical Characteristics

Meta Cases often emerge when:

- multiple Cases are compared
- recurring patterns become visible
- Open Questions repeatedly appear
- repository structures evolve in response to observation
- the observation process itself becomes the object of observation

---

## Current Scope

This layer remains exploratory.

The goal is not to establish definitive theory, but to preserve potentially significant higher-order observations for future investigation.

As additional Meta Cases accumulate, recurring structures may become visible that are difficult to detect from individual Cases alone.
