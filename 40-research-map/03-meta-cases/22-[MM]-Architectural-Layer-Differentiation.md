README files and research assets became clearly separated according to their organizational roles.

Paper Assembly Methodology emerged as an architectural methodology distinct from repository topology.
④ Difference from Ordinary Generation
The discussion did not evolve through theoretical expansion or manuscript writing.
Organizational architecture emerged through repository placement decisions.
Practical placement questions progressively clarified abstract architectural boundaries.
Repository management itself functioned as an observational process revealing higher-order organizational structure.
⑤ Self-observation Commentary
Repository placement unexpectedly became a mechanism for observing research architecture rather than merely organizing files.
Operational decisions consistently exposed increasingly abstract organizational layers.
The dialogue suggested that long-term repository management may itself serve as a source of architectural observation within the Research Program.
This observation strengthened the distinction between Topology (where assets belong) and Methodology (how research assets are produced), providing a more stable conceptual framework for future repository evolution.
Provisional Interpretation

This case suggests that repository placement can function as an architectural observation process, in which repeated operational decisions progressively reveal stable organizational layers within a long-horizon research program.
