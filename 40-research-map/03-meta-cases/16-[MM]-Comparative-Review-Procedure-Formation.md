# Comparative Review Procedure Formation

## Status

Meta Case (Provisional)

Methodological Layer

Version: v0.1

---

## Purpose

This document records a methodological observation in which the procedure of comparative review itself became an explicit object of observation.

The purpose is not to introduce a new analytical framework.

Instead, it documents how repeated Cluster Review gradually revealed an internal comparative procedure that can be reused across future methodological studies.

---

## Background

Earlier methodological observations primarily focused on:

- methodological architecture,
- observer position,
- rectification,
- analytical refinement,
- methodological governance.

During the comparative review of approximately twenty Crystallization Cases, however, attention gradually shifted toward the procedure by which comparative understanding itself emerged.

Rather than reviewing isolated Cases, the dialogue began revealing successive comparative units.

---

## Observed Emergence

The Cluster Review initially appeared to consist of a straightforward sequence:

```text
Case

↓

Cluster
```

As additional comparisons accumulated, this structure proved insufficient.

Further comparative layers naturally emerged:

```text
Case

↓

Cluster

↓

Capability

↓

Capability Organization

↓

Working Model
```

Importantly, these stages were not introduced beforehand.

Each successive layer emerged because comparative observation itself required an additional organizational level.

The comparative procedure therefore appeared to evolve recursively through observation.

---

## Methodological Significance

The primary observation was not the discovery of Capability Organization itself.

Instead, the dialogue demonstrated that comparative review possesses an observable developmental procedure.

The review process progressively generated new comparative units whenever the previous level no longer adequately organized accumulated observations.

Methodological refinement therefore occurred through procedural emergence rather than theoretical expansion.

---

## Working Interpretation

The present observation may be summarized as:

The procedure suggests that comparative review may naturally reorganize itself as observational complexity increases.
