## ② Prior Conditions

- Approximately twenty Crystallization Cases had already been comparatively reviewed.
- Comparative review had provisionally organized the recurring observations into:
  - Maintain
  - Transfer
  - Embed
- These concepts had initially been interpreted as Capability Organization.
- A scheduled rectification interval was completed before further methodological evaluation.

---

## ③ Observed Emergence

The discussion gradually shifted from evaluating the observational result toward examining the methodological process that produced the result.

Rather than introducing additional theoretical concepts, the dialogue reconstructed how the provisional explanation had emerged through repeated comparison across accumulated Cases.

The rectification interval revealed that refinement primarily occurred through repeated consistency evaluation rather than conceptual expansion.

Repeated comparison progressively reduced explanatory inconsistency while preserving the accumulated observational evidence.

As a result, the dialogue reached a revised methodological interpretation:

```text
Rectification

        ↓

Iterative Consistency Evaluation

        ↓

Conceptual Role Refinement

        ↓

Methodological Stabilization
```

## ⑤ Self-Observation Commentary

- The primary observation was not the emergence of a new concept, but the clarification of the methodological function of rectification itself.
- Rectification appeared to function as an iterative consistency evaluation process rather than as a period of conceptual expansion.
- Existing concepts gradually acquired more precise methodological roles without substantially increasing conceptual complexity.
- The transition from interpreting Maintain–Transfer–Embed as Capability Organization toward Minimum Observational Conditions illustrated this refinement process.
- The dialogue therefore documented not only a methodological result but also the process through which methodological structures progressively stabilize.
