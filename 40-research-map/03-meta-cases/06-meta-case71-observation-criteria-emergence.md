## Purpose

This document records the observed emergence of Observation Criteria as an independent methodological layer within the research program.

The purpose is not to introduce a new theoretical framework.

Instead, it documents how repeated cross-case structural comparison led to the explicit recognition of observation criteria prior to protocol construction.

---

## Background

Previous observations repeatedly demonstrated comparable developmental topology across independent contexts.

These included:

- Historical reconstruction
- Operational role evolution
- Cross-format projection
- Topology preservation analysis

As comparative observations accumulated, attention gradually shifted from individual observations toward the methodology underlying those observations.

---

## Central Observation

The primary transition was not the production of a new protocol.

Instead, the observation process itself became an explicit object of methodological organization.

A distinction naturally emerged between:

```text
Observation Criteria
↓

Observation Protocol
```

where:

- Observation Criteria defines **what should be observed**
- Observation Protocol defines **how observations should be performed**

This distinction appeared through repeated structural comparison rather than through prior theoretical planning.

---

## Methodological Transition

The observed developmental sequence may be summarized as:

```text
Repeated Structural Observation
        ↓
Cross-Case Comparison
        ↓
Invariant Extraction
        ↓
Observation Criteria
        ↓
Observation Protocol
```

Compared with ordinary procedural development,

```text
Observation
        ↓
Protocol
```

an intermediate methodological layer emerged through invariant extraction.

---

## Why This Is a Meta Case

Unlike ordinary Cases, this observation does not primarily concern:

- a structural phenomenon
- a repository operation
- a workflow
- an externalized artifact

Instead, it concerns the methodology used to observe those phenomena.

The primary observation object therefore shifted from research objects to observation methodology itself.

For this reason, the case is provisionally classified as a Meta Case.
