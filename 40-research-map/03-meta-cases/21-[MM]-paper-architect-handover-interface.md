# Purpose

This memo summarizes the completed Cluster Review conducted for the current Stage 2 (20-Crystallization) research project.

The objective is not to present theoretical conclusions.

The objective is to provide a stable observational foundation that can be used by the Paper Architect during manuscript development.

This memo therefore represents the final deliverable of the Cluster Analysis role.

---

# Scope

The review was conducted using approximately twenty representative Crystallization Cases.

The review followed the operational workflow:

Case Observation

↓

↓

Pattern Identification

↓

Cluster Formation

↓

↓

No manuscript construction was performed.

No chapter structure was considered.

No theoretical framework was introduced beyond the observational evidence.
