# Open Questions

This section organizes unresolved questions and research ambiguities
observed throughout the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning project.

The goal of this layer is to preserve:

- observational uncertainties
- phase-boundary ambiguities
- structural interpretation limits
- reproducibility questions
- projection-related problems
- cross-stage relationships

These questions are not treated as flaws,
but as active research targets within the evolving framework.

---

## Structure

### 20-Crystallization
Questions related to stabilization, semantic density,
persistent structures, and recursive organization.

### 30-Externalization
Questions related to projection, external structure formation,
research-space emergence, and public semantic organization.

### 35-Cross-Stage
Questions involving multiple stages, unclear boundaries,
or interactions between different structural layers.

---

## Notes

Many questions originate from concrete case studies
and ongoing dialogue observations.

The purpose is to preserve research traceability
rather than to provide finalized answers.
