# Research Map

This section contains exploratory structures, open problems,
research trajectories, and observational mapping layers
related to the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning program.

Unlike the paper layers, this section focuses on:

- unresolved questions
- structural ambiguities
- phase-boundary problems
- reproducibility conditions
- observational limitations
- cross-stage relationships

The purpose of this layer is not to present finalized theory,
but to preserve and organize the evolving research topology.

---

## Sections

### Open Questions
Collections of unresolved research questions emerging from
case studies, structural observations, and dialogue experiments.

### Research Notes
Lightweight notes documenting observations, mappings,
working hypotheses, and structural interpretations.
