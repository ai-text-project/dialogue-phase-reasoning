# Research Map

This section contains exploratory structures, open problems,
research trajectories, and observational mapping layers
related to the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning program.

Unlike the paper layers, this section focuses on:

- unresolved questions
- structural ambiguities
- phase-boundary problems
- reproducibility conditions
- observational limitations
- cross-stage relationships

The purpose of this layer is not to present finalized theory,
but to preserve and organize the evolving research topology.

---

## Sections

### Open Questions
Collections of unresolved research questions emerging from
case studies, structural observations, and dialogue experiments.

### Research Notes
Lightweight notes documenting observations, mappings,
working hypotheses, and structural interpretations.

---

## Current Status

This layer is under active development.

The structure may continue evolving as the research space expands.

The stage structure is not only a content classification, but also a recursive organizational principle across the repository.


---

## Future Development

Recent observations suggest that some structures within the Research Map layer may gradually evolve beyond the documentation of research content itself.

Examples include:

- Meta Cases
- Meta Observation
- Research Architecture
- Research-Space Evolution studies

These structures increasingly focus not only on observed phenomena, but also on the organization, interpretation, and development of the research program itself.

At present, these observations remain integrated within the Research Map layer.

Whether a dedicated Research Program Description layer will eventually emerge remains an open question.
