## Purpose

This document records the current placement principles used within the Research Map layer.

The purpose is not to define theoretical classifications.

Instead, the purpose is to support consistent placement of:

* Cases
* Research Notes
* Open Questions
* Meta Observations
* Operational Observations

across the repository structure.

---

## Core Observation

Repeated observation activities revealed that not all records describe the same type of object.

Different records may focus on:

* structures
* structure formation
* observation processes
* operational processes

As a result, placement decisions increasingly depend on identifying the **primary object being observed**.

# Current Placement Categories

## 1. Crystallization Layer

### Location

```text
20-crystallization/
```

### Primary Focus

* structural stabilization
* structure preservation
* crystallized organization
* reduced reconstruction cost

### Typical Examples

* stable conceptual structures
* structural clarification
* stabilization observations

---

## 2. Research Notes

### Location

```text
40-research-map/05-research-notes/
```

### Primary Focus

* open-question formation
* comparative exploration
* historical reconstruction
* exploratory interpretation

### Typical Examples

* Metastability and Crystallization
* Research Program Identity Question Formation
* Asset-Layer Community Alignment Question

These records are not considered finalized theoretical claims.

---

## 3. Emergence Observations

### Location

```text
40-research-map/00-emergence-observations/
```

### Primary Focus

* candidate cases
* unusual observations
* classification uncertainty
* emergence-related phenomena

### Typical Examples

* Recursive Crystallization Becoming an Observation Object
* External Theory Comparison and Open Question Formation
* Governance Interaction and Role Execution Drift

These records remain provisional until broader placement becomes clear.

---

## 4. Open Questions

### Location

```text
40-research-map/02-open-questions/
```

### Primary Focus

* unresolved questions
* placement uncertainty
* theoretical ambiguity
* future investigation targets

Open Questions are retained independently from Cases.

A Case may generate one or more Open Questions.

# Current Working Principle

When evaluating a new record, first identify:

> What is being observed?

If the answer is:

* a structure → Crystallization Layer
* a research question → Research Notes / Open Questions
* an observation process → Emergence Observations
* program evolution → Hub Repository

Placement should follow the **primary object of observation** rather than the surface topic.

---

# One-Line Summary

Research asset placement is determined by the primary object of observation rather than by topic, terminology, or repository location alone.
