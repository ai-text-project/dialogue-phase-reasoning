# Core Research Question

Are all recursive human–AI dialogue loops structurally equivalent?

Or can distinct loop topologies be identified?

Specifically:

- amplification-oriented recursive closure
- externally constrained structural convergence

---

# Preliminary Hypothesis

Two different classes of human–AI recursive interaction may exist.

## Type A

Sycophantic Amplification Loop

Characteristics:

- agreement reinforcement
- attractor fixation
- reduction of corrective pressure
- recursive closure

---

## Type B

Convergent Projection Loop

Characteristics:

- reflective stabilization
- external constraint preservation
- recursive externalization
- topology integration
- convergence through structure
