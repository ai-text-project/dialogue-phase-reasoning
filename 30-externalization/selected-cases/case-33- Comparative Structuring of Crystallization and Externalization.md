## Overview

This case documents a shift from individual case recording toward comparative observational structuring within the Dialogue-Phase Reasoning research environment.

Rather than focusing on a single externalization event, the discussion explored how accumulated cases may function as a structured observational dataset for examining relationships between:

- Crystallization (Stage 2)
- Externalization (Stage 3)

The focus gradually moved from documenting isolated observations toward developing a comparative methodology capable of supporting ongoing analysis across multiple cases.
