## ⑤ Self-Observation Commentary

The notable transition was not the creation of an individual diagram.

Rather, attention shifted toward the mechanism responsible for producing diagrams.

Repeated externalization activities generated sufficient stability to allow extraction of a reusable workflow.

The resulting artifact functions as a methodology layer rather than a figure-specific explanation.

This observation appears closer to:

Externalization

↓
